## Cross-reference observations

- Buyer confidence is built through different evidence systems: operational specificity in Laeyo, physical proof in Vitelle, institutional depth in Romano, brand authority in Créer, commercial accessibility in RainShadow Labs, and scientific longevity in YG Laboratories.
- The sources repeatedly separate route selection from detailed proof. Early buyer-path choices reduce ambiguity; later sections justify the promise with facility, process, range, standards, or history.
- Startup friendliness is strongest when commercial constraints and next steps are explicit. Premium positioning relies more on pacing, hierarchy, art direction, and selective claims.
- Product catalogs serve different jobs: evidence of breadth, a buyer-fit decision aid, a direct-commerce surface, or a gated conversion mechanism. These jobs should remain distinct in later analysis.
- Romano's blank hero capture and Laeyo's repeated fixed navigation are capture limitations, not reliable design evidence.

No final reusable-component selection has been made. Any later proposal must combine an approved buyer need with an original visual and technical treatment.
